    Text

    Text regions hold formatted prose with mathematics inside it. Their inline math is calculated like any math region.

    Text boxes and text blocks

    • A text box (Ctrl+T, Insert › Text box) widens as you type and wraps at the right margin. Drag its right edge to give it a fixed width, which it then keeps.
    • A text block (Ctrl+Shift+T, Insert › Text block) always spans the printable width of the page, from margin to margin, and grows downwards; the regions below move down with it.

    You can also start a text box by typing: on empty space, type a word and a Space. Because a word followed by a space cannot be the start of mathematics, Equavela turns the region into a text box and you can go on writing.

    Move a text region by dragging its border. A drag on the text selects text, as in a word processor. Esc leaves the region. A text box you empty and leave is removed.

    Formatting

    While a text region is being edited, the Text tab appears:

    • Font: bold (Ctrl+B), italic (Ctrl+I), underline (Ctrl+U), subscript, superscript and size (8 to 72 pt).
    • Paragraph: align left, centre, right or justify.
    • Text styles: Normal (10 pt), Title (28 pt), Subtitle (18 pt), Heading 1 (16 pt), Heading 2 (13 pt) and Heading 3 (12 pt). Headings appear in the Outline tab of the side panel.

    Enter starts a new paragraph. The text font is Noto Sans, bundled with Equavela, so text looks the same on every computer and in the PDF.

    Mathematics in text

    Press Ctrl+M in a text region to insert a math placeholder into the sentence, then type the math as in a math region. The arrow keys move into and out of it, and a click on it puts the caret inside. Backspace right after it or Delete right before it removes it.

    Inline math is calculated: a definition in a sentence, such as "the span is L := 6 m", defines L for everything below the text region, and "the moment is M =" shows the value in the sentence. A plain name or expression without := or = is only displayed.

    Pasting

    Plain text pasted on empty space makes a new text box. Math copied from Equavela and pasted into a text region becomes inline math.

    Spelling

    Misspelt words in text regions get a red wavy underline on screen; it is never printed. Right-click a word for up to six corrections, Ignore (remembered by the worksheet) or Add to dictionary (remembered for you in every worksheet). Mathematics, names, Julia code, plots and tables are never checked.

    The worksheet has one language, English by default, and a text region can have its own. Edit › Spelling… sets them and can download any of the 96 LibreOffice dictionaries after you agree; downloaded dictionaries can be removed again. Spell checking can be turned off in the options.
