    Functions

    Descriptive statistics

    contingtbl corr covar cvar Ftest gmean hmean kendltau kendltau2 kurt mean median mode percentile skew Spear stdev Stdev var Var

    contingtbl

    contingtbl(M)

    Chi-squared, dof, probability, Cramer's V, contingency coefficient.

    Julia
    Distributions.Chisq
    Notes
    For a contingency table: Pearson's χ², the degrees of freedom, the upper-tail probability, Cramér's V and the contingency coefficient C.

    corr

    corr(A, B)

    Pearson correlation coefficient.

    Julia
    Statistics.cor
    Notes
    Pearson's correlation coefficient cvar(A, B)/(stdev(A)·stdev(B)); the same value as Julia's cor.

    covar

    covar(vx, vy)

    Covariance of two vectors (page covariance.html).

    Julia
    Statistics.cov
    Notes
    The same formula as cvar, for two vectors.

    cvar

    cvar(A, B)

    Covariance of A and B (divides by m*n; conjugates).

    Julia
    Statistics.cov
    Notes
    Σ(aᵢ − ā)·conj(bᵢ − b̄)/N: the population covariance, dividing by N, over all elements of two arrays of equal size. Julia's cov divides by N − 1 unless corrected=false.

    Ftest

    Ftest(v1, v2)

    F-test statistic and probability for equal variance.

    Julia
    Distributions.FDist
    Notes
    The vector [F; p]: the ratio of the two sample variances and the two-sided probability from the F distribution with (n₁ − 1, n₂ − 1) degrees of freedom.

    gmean

    gmean(A, B, C, …)

    Geometric mean (positive reals).

    Notes
    The geometric mean of positive numbers without units, (∏xᵢ)^(1/n), computed through logarithms when the product would overflow.

    hmean

    hmean(A, B, C, …)

    Harmonic mean (positive reals).

    Notes
    The harmonic mean n/Σ(1/xᵢ) of positive numbers; units are kept.

    kendltau

    kendltau(v1, v2)

    Kendall's tau-b, the z-score of the normal approximation with the tie correction of its variance, and the two-sided probability, as SciPy.

    Notes
    Kendall's τb, its z-score from the normal approximation with the tie correction of the variance, and the two-sided probability, as SciPy's kendalltau.

    kendltau2

    kendltau2(M)

    Kendall's tau for a contingency table M.

    Notes
    Kendall's τb from a contingency table of frequencies, with its z-score and two-sided probability (without tie correction of the variance).

    kurt

    kurt(A, B, C, …)

    Kurtosis.

    Notes
    The excess sample kurtosis with the small-sample correction used by spreadsheets. StatsBase.kurtosis returns the unadjusted excess kurtosis.

    mean

    mean(A, B, C, …)

    Arithmetic mean over all elements of the arguments.

    Julia
    Statistics.mean
    Notes
    The arithmetic mean over every element of every argument, scalars and arrays together (Julia's mean takes one collection). Units are kept and must agree.

    median

    median(A, B, C, …)

    Median (real values).

    Julia
    Statistics.median
    Notes
    Over every element of every argument; real values only; the mean of the two middle values for an even count.

    mode

    mode(A, B, C, …)

    Most frequent value.

    Notes
    The most frequent value; when several values are equally frequent, the smallest. Real values only.

    percentile

    percentile(v, p)

    Value below which fraction p (0..1) of v lies.

    Julia
    Statistics.quantile
    Notes
    The value below which the fraction p (0 to 1) of the data lies, interpolating linearly between order statistics at position p·(n − 1). This matches Julia's default quantile(v, p).

    skew

    skew(A, B, C, …)

    Skewness.

    Notes
    The sample skewness n/((n−1)(n−2))·Σz³ with z standardised by the sample standard deviation (the adjusted Fisher–Pearson coefficient, as spreadsheets compute it). Julia's StatsBase.skewness returns the unadjusted value.

    Spear

    Spear(v1, v2)

    Spearman rank correlation, returns 5-element vector.

    Notes
    Spearman's rank correlation as a vector of five values: the sum of squared rank differences D, its normal score and two-sided probability, ρ, and the probability of ρ from Student's t distribution. Ties get average ranks.

    stdev

    stdev(A, B, C, …)

    Population standard deviation.

    Julia
    Statistics.std
    Notes
    The population standard deviation (divides by N), Julia's std(x, corrected=false). Julia's default std corresponds to Stdev.

    Stdev

    Stdev(A, B, C, …)

    Sample standard deviation.

    Julia
    Statistics.std
    Notes
    The sample standard deviation (divides by N − 1), Julia's default std.

    var

    var(A, B, C, …)

    Population variance (divides by N).

    Julia
    Statistics.var
    Notes
    The population variance, dividing by N, which is Julia's var(x, corrected=false); Julia's default var divides by N − 1 (that is Var here). Complex values use |x − mean|².

    Var

    Var(A, B, C, …)

    Sample variance.

    Julia
    Statistics.var
    Notes
    The sample variance, dividing by N − 1: Julia's default var.
