    Units reference

    Every unit Equavela knows, grouped by dimension. Names are case sensitive. A unit marked prefixes also exists with every SI prefix: p (10^-12), n (10^-9), μ (10^-6), u (10^-6), m (10^-3), c (10^-2), d (10^-1), k (10^3), M (10^6), G (10^9), T (10^12), so km, kN, MPa and μs are units.
