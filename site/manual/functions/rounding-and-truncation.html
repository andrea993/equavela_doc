    Rounding and truncation

    ceil Ceil floor Floor round Round trunc Trunc

    ceil

    ceil(z)

    Smallest integer >= z.

    Julia
    Base.ceil
    Notes
    As floor.

    Ceil

    Ceil(z, y)

    Smallest multiple of y >= z.

    Julia
    Base.ceil
    Notes
    ceil(z/y)·y.

    floor

    floor(z)

    Greatest integer <= z.

    Julia
    Base.floor
    Notes
    Element by element; a complex number is rounded part by part. Units are kept.

    Floor

    Floor(z, y)

    Greatest multiple of y <= z.

    Julia
    Base.floor
    Notes
    floor(z/y)·y: the greatest multiple of y not above z. Useful with units: Floor(1.234 m, 5 cm) = 1.2 m.

    round

    round(z, [n])

    z rounded to n decimals (default 0).

    Julia
    Base.round
    Notes
    round(z, n) rounds to n decimals (default 0; negative n rounds to tens, hundreds…). Halves round away from zero (round(2.5) = 3), whereas Julia's default rounds halves to even (round(2.5) = 2.0); this is Julia's RoundNearestTiesAway.

    Round

    Round(z, y)

    round(z/y)*y, nearest multiple of y.

    Julia
    Base.round
    Notes
    round(z/y)·y with halves away from zero.

    trunc

    trunc(z)

    Integer part (fraction removed).

    Julia
    Base.trunc
    Notes
    As floor: rounds towards zero.

    Trunc

    Trunc(z, y)

    trunc(z/y)*y.

    Julia
    Base.trunc
    Notes
    trunc(z/y)·y.
