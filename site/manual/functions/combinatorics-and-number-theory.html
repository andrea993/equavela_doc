    Functions

    Combinatorics and number theory

    combin gcd lcm mod permut

    combin

    combin(n, k)

    Number of combinations, n >= k >= 0.

    Julia
    Base.binomial
    Notes
    The binomial coefficient, computed exactly with big integers and returned as a double. Needs integers with n ≥ k ≥ 0.

    gcd

    gcd(A, B, C, …)

    Greatest common divisor of dimensionless integers.

    Julia
    Base.gcd
    Notes
    Of any number of integers or integer arrays; dimensionless only; the result is non-negative.

    lcm

    lcm(A, B, C, …)

    Least common multiple.

    Julia
    Base.lcm
    Notes
    As gcd.

    mod

    mod(x, y)

    Remainder of x/y with the sign of x.

    Julia
    Base.rem
    Notes
    The remainder of x/y with the sign of x (truncated division), which is Julia's rem, not Julia's mod (which takes the sign of y): mod(−7, 3) = −1. Units must agree; y must not be zero. Element by element.

    permut

    permut(n, k)

    Number of permutations.

    Julia
    Base.factorial
    Notes
    The number of ordered selections of k out of n, n!/(n − k)!.
