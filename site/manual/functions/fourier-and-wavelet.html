    Functions

    Fourier and wavelet

    Bspline cfft cpt dft dftr icfft idft idftr iwave wave

    Bspline

    Bspline(m, n)

    Analysis and synthesis spline wavelet filters.

    Notes
    The analysis and synthesis filters of the spline wavelet of orders (m, n), as a two-column matrix. Only the pairs of the standard table are accepted.

    cfft

    cfft(v)

    Deprecated legacy name, replaced by dft (still works in the reference application).

    Julia
    FFTW.ifft
    Notes
    An older transform convention: the positive exponent, scaled by 1/√n, that is ifft(x)·√n. Kept for older worksheets; use dft for new work.

    cpt

    cpt(a, n)

    Local cosine packet table, n levels.

    Julia
    FFTW.r2r
    Notes
    The local cosine packet table: a column of n + 1 vectors, levels 0 to n. Level l cuts the periodic signal into 2l blocks, folds it smoothly at the block boundaries and applies an orthonormal DCT-IV (FFTW.r2r(…, REDFT11)) to each block. The length must be a multiple of 2n+1; matrices are transformed along columns, then rows.

    dft

    dft(A)

    Forward Fourier transform of a complex vector or matrix.

    Julia
    FFTW.fft
    Notes
    The discrete Fourier transform Σ x[k]·e^(−2πi·jk/n) without normalisation, exactly fft(x). A matrix is transformed along both dimensions. The result keeps the shape of the input; units are kept. Element 0 of the result is the zero frequency.

    dftr

    dftr(B)

    Forward Fourier transform of real data.

    Julia
    FFTW.fft
    Notes
    The transform of real data: only the non-negative frequencies, n/2 + 1 values for n samples, as Julia's rfft. The length (or the number of columns of a matrix) must be even; imaginary parts are ignored.

    icfft

    icfft(v)

    Deprecated legacy name, replaced by idft.

    Julia
    FFTW.fft
    Notes
    The inverse of cfft: fft(x)/√n.

    idft

    idft(Z)

    Inverse of dft.

    Julia
    FFTW.ifft
    Notes
    The inverse of dft, dividing by the number of elements, as ifft.

    idftr

    idftr(Z)

    Inverse of dftr.

    Julia
    FFTW.ifft
    Notes
    The inverse of dftr: rebuilds the even-length real signal from the non-negative frequencies, as irfft(z, 2(length(z) − 1)).

    iwave

    iwave(u)

    Inverse wavelet transform.

    Notes
    The exact inverse of wave.

    wave

    wave(v)

    Daubechies four-coefficient wavelet transform.

    Notes
    The discrete wavelet transform with the four-coefficient Daubechies wavelet, as a pyramid down to two smooth coefficients. The length must be a power of two. Implemented directly; Julia's Wavelets.jl orders the coefficients differently.
