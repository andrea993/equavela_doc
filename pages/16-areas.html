Areas

An area groups the regions between a start line and an end line under a title. Collapse it to hide intermediate steps, for example a long derivation, while keeping its definitions available below.

Inserting an area

Insert › Area adds an expanded area at the crosshair, titled "Area", 120 pt high, across the printable width. The regions whose top lies between its start line and its end line belong to it. Drag the end line's handle to make the area larger or smaller; an area never becomes shorter than its content, and it may continue across pages. Double-click the title to rename it.

Collapsing

Click the triangle on the start line, or use the Area tab, to collapse or expand the area. A collapsed area shows only its title line; the regions below it move up, on screen and in print. Collapsing never changes calculation: the regions inside are still calculated and their definitions are visible below.

Moving, copying and deleting

Dragging the start line moves the area with all its regions, also while collapsed. Copying an area copies its regions with it. Deleting an area deletes its regions too, in one undo step. Inserting or removing lines inside an area keeps the change inside the area.
