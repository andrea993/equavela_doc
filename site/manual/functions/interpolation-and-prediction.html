    Functions

    Interpolation and prediction

    Bicubic2D Binterp bspline cspline DWS interp linterp lspline polycoeff polyint polyiter predict pspline rationalint Spline2 Thiele Thielecoeff

    Bicubic2D

    Bicubic2D(vx, vy, Z, p, q)

    Bicubic interpolation at (p, q).

    Notes
    Interpolates the grid Z (Z[i, j] at (vx[i], vy[j])) at the point (p, q) with natural cubic splines, first along y, then along x; it extrapolates linearly outside.

    Binterp

    Binterp(x, b)

    B-spline value (and derivatives) from Spline2 output.

    Notes
    The value and the derivatives at x of the B-spline made by Spline2.

    bspline

    bspline(vx, vy, u, n)

    B-spline of degree n with knots u.

    Notes
    A B-spline of degree n that passes through the points, with the knots u (rows(vx) − n + 1 ascending values covering the data; the end knots are repeated). For use with interp.

    cspline

    cspline(vx, vy) / cspline(Mxy, Mz)

    Cubic spline coefficients with cubic end conditions (vs for interp).

    Notes
    The coefficient vector of an interpolating cubic spline whose ends are cubic (the second derivative is linear near the ends), for use with interp. The vector holds a header and the second derivatives at the data points. vx must ascend.

    DWS

    DWS(b)

    Durbin-Watson statistic of Spline2 output.

    Notes
    The Durbin–Watson statistic of the residuals of a Spline2 fit.

    interp

    interp(vs, vx, vy, x) / interp(vs, Mxy, Mz, X)

    Interpolated value from the vs produced by cspline, lspline, pspline, bspline or loess.

    Notes
    Evaluates at x the curve described by a vector made by cspline, lspline, pspline, bspline or loess for the same data: interp(cspline(vx, vy), vx, vy, x). Outside the data the end pieces continue (extrapolation). x may be an array. For two-dimensional data use the matrix form interp(vs, Mxy, Mz, X). Julia's Interpolations.jl works with interpolation objects instead of coefficient vectors.

    linterp

    linterp(vx, vy, x)

    Linear interpolation.

    Notes
    Linear interpolation of the points (vx, vy) at x; outside the data the end segments are extended. vx must ascend. x may be an array.

    lspline

    lspline(vx, vy) / lspline(Mxy, Mz)

    Cubic spline, linear end condition.

    Notes
    As cspline, with a natural end condition: the curve is straight at the ends (second derivative zero).

    polycoeff

    polycoeff(vx, vy)

    Coefficients of the interpolating polynomial.

    Notes
    The coefficients, constant term first, of the polynomial of degree n − 1 through the n points. The inverse of evaluating a polynomial; compare Polynomials.fit in Julia.

    polyint

    polyint(vx, vy, x)

    Polynomial interpolation at x and expected error.

    Notes
    Polynomial interpolation through all points by Neville's algorithm, starting at the point nearest to x: the vector [value; error], the error being the signed last correction.

    polyiter

    polyiter(vx, vy, x, N, e)

    Polynomial interpolation with max order N and max error e.

    Notes
    The column [converged; order; value]: Neville's interpolation of increasing order up to N, stopping when the correction is at most e times the value.

    predict

    predict(v, m, n)

    n values predicted past the end of v (linear prediction, m coefficients).

    Notes
    n values that continue the evenly spaced data v, by Burg's (maximum entropy) linear prediction with m coefficients.

    pspline

    pspline(vx, vy) / pspline(Mxy, Mz)

    Cubic spline, parabolic end condition.

    Notes
    As cspline, with parabolic ends.

    rationalint

    rationalint(vx, vy, x)

    Rational function interpolation and error.

    Notes
    Rational function interpolation (Bulirsch–Stoer) at x: [value; error]. A pole is an error.

    Spline2

    Spline2(vx, vy, n, [vw], [u], [level])

    Optimal order-n B-spline knots.

    Notes
    A least-squares B-spline of degree n. Without knots, equally spaced knots are added until the Durbin–Watson statistic of the residuals reaches 2; with u, the given knots are used. vw gives the standard deviation of each point. The result is used by Binterp and DWS.

    Thiele

    Thiele(vx, coeff, x)

    Thiele interpolation at x.

    Notes
    Evaluates Thiele's continued fraction a₀ + (x − x₀)/(a₁ + (x − x₁)/(a₂ + …)) at x.

    Thielecoeff

    Thielecoeff(vx, vy)

    Thiele continued fraction coefficients.

    Notes
    The coefficients of Thiele's continued fraction through the points, for Thiele.
