    Functions

    Regression and curve fitting

    confidence expfit genfit intercept LeastSquaresFit lgsfit line linfit lnfit loess logfit medfit multidfit polyfit polyfitc polyfitstat pwrfit rationalfit rationalfitnp sinfit slope stderr

    confidence

    confidence(vx, vy, F, b, conf)

    Confidence limits of fit parameters b.

    Notes
    For fitted parameters b of F(x, u): the half-width of the conf confidence interval of each parameter (first column) and the Student t value used.

    expfit

    expfit(vx, vy, [vg])

    Coefficients of A*exp(b*x) + C.

    Notes
    The coefficients [A; b; C] of A·e^(b·x) + C; the guess vg is optional.

    genfit

    genfit(vx, vy, vg, F)

    Levenberg-Marquardt nonlinear fit of F(x, u) with guess vg.

    Notes
    Fits the parameters u of a function F(x, u) by Levenberg–Marquardt, starting at vg. F may return the value followed by its partial derivatives; otherwise they are computed numerically. Compare LsqFit.curve_fit in Julia.

    intercept

    intercept(vx, vy)

    Intercept of the least-squares line.

    Notes
    The intercept of the least-squares line.

    LeastSquaresFit

    LeastSquaresFit(vx, vy, F, guess, conf, [Stdy], [LBUB], [Acc])

    Nonlinear fit with parameter confidence limits.

    Notes
    Fits the scalar function F(x, u) and returns, per parameter, [value, lower, upper] with conf confidence limits. Optional: the standard deviations of y (a vector), bounds (a two-column matrix) and the accuracy (a scalar), recognised by their shape.

    lgsfit

    lgsfit(vx, vy, vg)

    Coefficients of a/(1 + b*exp(-c*x)).

    Notes
    The coefficients of the logistic curve a/(1 + b·e^(−c·x)).

    line

    line(vx, vy)

    Vector [a; b] for the least-squares line a + b*x.

    Notes
    The least-squares line as [intercept; slope].

    linfit

    linfit(vx, vy, F)

    Least-squares coefficients for a linear combination of the functions in F.

    Notes
    The least-squares coefficients of a linear combination of the basis functions returned (as a column) by F(x). Julia equivalent: build the design matrix and solve with \.

    lnfit

    lnfit(vx, vy)

    Logarithmic fit (named on the category page; no detail signature found).

    Notes
    The coefficients [a; b] of a·ln(x) + b (a linear fit in ln x).

    loess

    loess(vx, vy, span) / loess(Mx, vy, span)

    Localized second-order polynomial regression.

    Notes
    Local quadratic regression with tricube weights over the nearest span·N points, for use with interp. Works with one variable (vectors) or several (a matrix of points).

    logfit

    logfit(vx, vy, vg)

    Coefficients of a*ln(x + b) + c.

    Notes
    The coefficients of a·ln(x + b) + c.

    medfit

    medfit(vx, vy)

    Median-median line [a; b].

    Notes
    Tukey's resistant median–median line, [intercept; slope].

    multidfit

    multidfit(X, Y, G, F)

    General multivariate fit.

    Notes
    As genfit for several independent variables: the rows of X are passed to F as column vectors.

    polyfit

    polyfit(X, Y, n/"terms"/M)

    Function for a multivariate polynomial regression surface.

    Notes
    Multivariate polynomial regression. The third argument is the degree (all terms up to that total degree), a string naming the terms ("A,AA": A the first variable, AA its square, AB the product with the second), or a matrix of exponents. The result is a function of one argument: a number for one variable, a vector of the variables otherwise.

    polyfitc

    polyfitc(X, Y, n/"terms"/M, [conf])

    Regression coefficients with extra information.

    Notes
    The coefficient table of polyfit: a header row and one row per term with coefficient, standard error, confidence limits, variance inflation factor, t value and one-sided p value.

    polyfitstat

    polyfitstat(X, Y, n/"terms"/M, [conf])

    Statistics of the polynomial regression model.

    Notes
    The statistics of polyfit: standard deviation, R², adjusted and predicted R², PRESS, Durbin–Watson, the coefficient table, the ANOVA table and per-observation diagnostics (leverage, studentised residuals, Cook's distance, DFFITS).

    pwrfit

    pwrfit(vx, vy, vg)

    Coefficients of a*x^b + c.

    Notes
    The coefficients of a·x^b + c.

    rationalfit

    rationalfit(vx, vy, conf, m, n, [Stdy], [LBUB], [Acc], ["noscale"])

    Rational polynomial fit, parameters with confidence limits.

    Notes
    Fits (a₀ + … + aₘxᵐ)/(1 + b₁x + … + bₙxⁿ) by Levenberg–Marquardt; each row is a parameter [value, lower, upper] with conf confidence limits (rows a₀…aₘ, then b₁…bₙ).

    rationalfitnp

    rationalfitnp(vx, vy, conf, m, n, [Stdy], [LBUB], [Acc], ["noscale"])

    Like rationalfit, avoiding poles.

    Notes
    As rationalfit, penalising a denominator that approaches zero inside the data range, so the fit has no poles there.

    sinfit

    sinfit(vx, vy, vg)

    Coefficients of a*sin(x + b) + c.

    Notes
    The coefficients of a·sin(x + b) + c.

    slope

    slope(vx, vy)

    Slope of the least-squares line.

    Notes
    The slope of the least-squares line.

    stderr

    stderr(vx, vy)

    Standard error of the linear regression.

    Notes
    The standard error of the linear regression, √(Σresiduals²/(n − 2)).
