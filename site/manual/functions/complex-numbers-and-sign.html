    Functions

    Complex numbers and sign

    abs angle arg atan2 csgn Im Re sign signum

    abs

    abs(x)

    Absolute value of a number, magnitude of a complex number, or element-wise for arrays.

    Julia
    Base.abs
    Notes
    Absolute value or complex modulus, element by element on arrays. The operator |x| differs on arrays: it gives the length of a vector and the determinant of a square matrix.

    angle

    angle(x, y)

    Polar angle of point (x, y), 0 to 2*pi (excl.).

    Julia
    Base.atan
    Notes
    The angle of the point (x, y) in [0, 2π), measured counter-clockwise from the positive x axis; Julia's atan(y, x) plus 2π when negative. (0, 0) is an error.

    arg

    arg(z)

    Principal argument, -pi to pi inclusive.

    Julia
    Base.angle
    Notes
    The principal argument in (−π, π], as Julia's angle; arg(0) = 0. Units are ignored.

    atan2

    atan2(x, y)

    Angle of point (x, y), -pi to pi (excl. -pi).

    Julia
    Base.atan
    Notes
    atan2(x, y) is Julia's atan(y, x): note that x comes first here and second in Julia. The result is in (−π, π]; both arguments need the same unit; (0, 0) is an error.

    csgn

    csgn(z)

    Half-plane of z: 0 at 0, 1 for right half-plane (or on positive imaginary axis), -1 otherwise.

    Notes
    1 when z is in the right half-plane or on the positive imaginary axis, −1 otherwise, 0 at 0.

    Im

    Im(Z)

    Imaginary part.

    Julia
    Base.imag
    Notes
    Element by element.

    Re

    Re(Z)

    Real part of a scalar or matrix.

    Julia
    Base.real
    Notes
    Element by element.

    sign

    sign(x)

    Sign of a real number: -1, 0, 1.

    Julia
    Base.sign
    Notes
    −1, 0 or 1 for a real number; units are ignored. Complex numbers are an error (use signum).

    signum

    signum(z, [x])

    Vectorized sign of a real or complex number.

    Julia
    Base.sign
    Notes
    z/|z|, also for complex z (as Julia's sign of a complex number); at 0 it returns the optional second argument, 1 by default, where Julia returns 0.
